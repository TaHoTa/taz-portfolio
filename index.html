About

Civil engineer and development professional with more than seven years of progressively responsible experience managing humanitarian and development programmes in Bangladesh. Experience spans shelter, WASH, disaster risk reduction, climate resilience, settlement planning and community infrastructure - leading donor-funded programmes from design and resource mobilization through implementation, monitoring and reporting for UNHCR, IOM, the European Union, government authorities, NGOs and communities.
